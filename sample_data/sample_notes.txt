Autonomous Multi-Agent Systems & Algorithmic Optimization Notes

Key Observations:
1. Traditional monolithic software systems suffer from synchronous execution bottlenecks under heavy analytical loads.
2. Decoupling agent operations into dedicated layers (Ingestion, Code Analysis, Research Grounding, Synthesis, and Output Generation) significantly reduces latency.
3. Combining AST static inspection with peer-reviewed arXiv citations guarantees that generated technical manuscripts remain mathematically grounded and zero-hallucination.
4. IEEE and ACM publication standards mandate rigorous citation formatting and formal complexity analysis tables.
